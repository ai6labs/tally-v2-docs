Tally saves the PDF attached to a Gmail receipt, then shows those documents on **Subscriptions**, in the **Invoices** view.

## On this Mac

After you turn on Gmail and a scan finds a receipt with a PDF, Tally keeps that file in your Tally data folder (`.tally/tracker/invoices/`). Emails without a PDF are not saved. The email's text is never stored.

Open **Settings**, then **Local data**, then **Saved invoices** to see the count, open the folder, or delete the local PDFs. Deleting them does not remove the receipts in Gmail. Tally saves them again on the next check while Gmail is on.

## In your Tally cloud

When you are signed in, Tally keeps a copy of **approved** invoice PDFs in private storage in your Tally account by default. Approved means you added that subscription, or Tally saved the receipt without review because it came from the service's verified sender and every check passed. Items waiting for review and services you skipped are not uploaded. Turning the switch off stops new copies; it does not delete copies already there, which is a separate choice in Privacy.

The Privacy switch is **Sync documents and saved invoices to your account**. It is on by default. An explicit off stays off.

- Turn it off in **Settings**, then **Privacy**, to stop further uploads.
- When you turn it off, Tally asks whether to delete files already in your Tally cloud. That box is unticked by default, so existing copies stay unless you tick it.
- Erasing captured data on this Mac does not delete cloud copies.

You must be signed in for a cloud copy. Signed out, invoices stay only on this Mac.

## The Invoices view

On **Subscriptions**, open **Invoices**. You get:

- totals for billed amount, documents saved, and items that need review,
- a monthly chart, one currency at a time,
- lists **By month** or **By service**,
- **Invoice** and **Receipt** chips to download a PDF,
- **Open in Gmail**, and
- **Review details**, which corrects date, amount, currency or status on this Mac. The PDF itself is not changed.

**Download all PDFs** and **Export CSV** sit above the list.

## Read a file yourself

**Add**, then **Read invoices**, reads a PDF or image you choose into drafts. Check the drafts before you save them. Sending a file to a model is off until you turn **Read with AI** on; that is separate from cloud copies of Gmail PDFs.
