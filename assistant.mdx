Open **Ask Tally** in the app navigation, or visit [Ask Tally](https://app.tallyless.com/assistant). The assistant uses your current personal account or device scope. Signing in enables AI and private account storage. A team page does not grant access to teammates' private invoices.

## Understand your spending

Choose **Explain my spending** or ask a question. Tally keeps monthly plan amounts separate from saved invoice amounts, vendor-reported payments and credits. These figures do not establish bank settlement or complete spending coverage. Each currency remains separate.

Financial replies use checked figures and fixed explanations. The model interprets the question and reads supported invoices; it cannot approve arbitrary account changes or invent payment status.

## Read an invoice

Choose **Review an invoice** and select a PDF, PNG, JPEG or WebP file, up to 3 MB. Selecting the file sends it to the configured AI service. Never paste passwords or API keys into the conversation.

Check the extracted vendor, invoice number, date, currency, amount, seats and line items against **Open original**. Tally explains why a finding needs review. Missing fields, uncertain payment status, adjustments and conflicting evidence require attention. You can correct fields before choosing **Save as reviewed evidence**.

For a link requiring sign-in or an expired download, open the provider's billing page and download the original yourself. The assistant does not introduce a new background mailbox or portal collector.

## Automatic saves and private copies

Open **Assistant preferences**. To allow automatic saves, enable **Save verified invoice findings automatically** and **Keep invoice originals and extracted fields in my private Tally storage**.

Automatic saving requires document and arithmetic checks; a model confidence score alone is insufficient. Image-only findings stay in review. On the local app, the existing Privacy setting for account invoice copies must also permit storage.

Saved evidence appears on the Assistant page. It does not create a recurring subscription or count an invoice and its matching receipt as two charges. **Undo saved finding** excludes the obligation from assistant analysis and retains its originals and audit record. A suppressed obligation cannot be silently re-added.

## Monitor renewals

Enable **Monitor this space** in **Assistant preferences**. The page shows known upcoming renewals and billing dates that need checking. Dismiss a finding or snooze it for a day.

This checks available subscription data while Tally is running, with refresh on opening or returning to the app. It does not create an always-on cloud mailbox monitor. Check **Last checked** and source coverage; unavailable or stale data does not mean there are no bills.

## Conversation privacy

Messages stay in the app during the session and across in-app navigation. **Clear conversation**, sign-out, a changed account/data scope or refreshing the app clears them. Clearing a conversation does not delete saved originals. The model key stays on the InsForge backend.

## Product help and connections

Ask about Tally setup, Gmail permissions, invoice storage, syncing or installation. Product replies cite the customer help page they use. The assistant stays within Tally documentation and the account facts provided to it; it does not answer unrelated general questions or guess missing financial details.

The documented AI-agent setup covers Claude Desktop, Claude Code and Claude on the web. A ChatGPT connection is not verified by the current Tally guide; do not assume generic MCP instructions establish support. See [Connect AI agents](https://app.tallyless.com/guides/connect-ai-agents.html).

The [Chrome extension preview](/chrome-extension) is available as a ZIP for manual installation. It is not a Chrome Web Store release, and downloading the package does not verify deployed account authorization.
