Open **Subscriptions → Seats** to compare recorded seats bought with seats in use. The source of each number matters: an invoice quantity, an assignment and measured activity answer different questions.

## Read the source label

| Label | What to check |
|---|---|
| **Measured** | Which connected provider and activity window supplied the reading. |
| **Assigned** | A seat is assigned; that does not by itself establish recent use. |
| **Entered by you** | The quantity came from a local record and needs updating when the plan changes. |
| **Not available** | Tally has no usable activity measurement; do not interpret this as zero. |
| **0 active users** | A reading reported zero for its measured window; check coverage and account before acting. |

Activity can be shown over 30, 60 or 90 days when the source supplies it. “Last login” and “last activity” are different signals, and missing activity does not prove a paid seat is unused.

## Check seats read from an invoice

In an invoice import, compare the proposed seats with the printed plan and line items. Tally can recognize stated quantities, but an assumed default of 1 is not confirmation. Conflicting quantities require your review.

A bill can contain base seats, extra seats, usage units, credits, discounts and prorations. Do not treat every quantity as people or licenses. The saved-invoice review currently corrects billing date, amount, currency and status; it does not expose every line item for editing.

## Connect an account when needed

Supported company account connectors can supply member, seat and activity information when you have the required provider access. Availability depends on the provider, plan, permissions and connection health. An unavailable reading is not an empty team.

See [Connecting sources](/connecting-sources). Never paste an ordinary inference key where the setup asks for an admin or management key.

## Before removing a seat

Check the owner, account, billing cycle and whether the person uses another device or a different workspace. A proposed saving is an estimate until you change the provider plan and confirm its billing effect.

Changing or pausing a Tally record does not remove provider access or cancel a paid seat. Complete any change in the provider account and retain the confirmation.
