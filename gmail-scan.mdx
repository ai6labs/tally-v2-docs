Tally's Gmail subscription scan looks for billing evidence and suggests services to add. It runs on your Mac with read-only access. A connected inbox or an empty result does not establish that every invoice has been collected.

## Start a scan

1. Choose **Scan Gmail** during setup, on Home's scan card, or from **Subscriptions → Add**.
2. Complete Google sign-in and the requested read-only permission.
3. Return to Tally. You can use the app while the scan runs.
4. Review the results. **Add** saves the proposed subscriptions you choose; **Review** opens findings that need checking.

Signing in to Tally with Google does not by itself grant access to Gmail. If Gmail is unavailable in your copy, use [manual entry or imports](/subscriptions).

## Understand progress

The scan checks recent receipts first, covering the last 90 days, then older history up to roughly 400 days. This helps find annual plans. Processing is paced, and a large mailbox can take time.

An interrupted scan can resume after Tally restarts or reconnects. Keep Tally running and connected to the internet. Background rescans are scheduled about every six hours while the local service runs; they do not run while your Mac is off.

If a scan is paused, rate-limited or incomplete, retain the findings already shown and follow the retry guidance. A cap or partial history means coverage is incomplete, even if some subscriptions were found. Older receipts can be [imported manually](/importing-invoices).

## Review findings

Compare the merchant, plan, amount, currency, date and billing period with the original email. **Open in Gmail** opens the source message when its reference is available.

Missing history does not mean a service is unused or canceled. A failed-payment notice does not establish a successful payment, and a trial or sign-up email does not establish an active paid plan.

## Saved PDFs

After you agree to the invoice-retention wording, Tally can save PDF attachments from receipts found by the subscription scan on this Mac. Open **Subscriptions → Invoices** to see the saved documents and source emails.

This background collection does not retrieve every invoice hidden behind a billing-portal link. For a public invoice link or a file you downloaded yourself, use [Read invoices](/importing-invoices). If a PDF is missing, see [Troubleshoot invoice scans](/invoice-troubleshooting).

## Rescan or disconnect

Open **Settings → Email** to rescan or disconnect Gmail. Disconnecting stops future access; retained results and files have separate deletion controls. Review [Privacy](/privacy) before deleting data.
