Use **Subscriptions** to keep the plans you pay for in one place. You can add a plan yourself, review a Gmail finding, or import a statement or invoice. Check the amount, currency and billing cycle before adding a record.

## Choose how to add a plan

Open **Subscriptions**, then **Add**:

| Option | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **Add manually** | You know the plan and billing details. |
| **Scan Gmail** | You want Tally to look for subscription receipts. |
| **Import statement (CSV)** | You have a bank or card statement to match against your plans. |
| **Other imports…** | You have invoice files, a public invoice link, a subscription list or email text. |

Gmail findings and imported drafts need your confirmation. A service name, an installed app or a sign-up email does not establish a paid subscription.

## Check a subscription

Select a row to open its details. Check the service, plan, amount, currency, billing cycle and next billing date. Review its history and use **Open in Gmail**, when available, to compare the original receipt.

For an annual plan, keep the annual billed amount and renewal date clear. A monthly equivalent helps compare plans; it does not mean the provider charges you every month.

Use **Calendar** to see recorded renewal dates. A missing receipt or unknown renewal date does not establish that billing has stopped.

## Check possible duplicates

Before removing a record, compare the account, product, plan, billing channel and invoice reference. The same service can be paid through Apple and directly, or belong to two different workspaces.

An invoice and the receipt paying that invoice can describe one charge. Two separate subscriptions to the same service can describe two real charges. A free benefit can also exist alongside a paid plan; check the provider before deciding it replaces that plan.

See [Review invoices and receipts](/invoices) for document checks.

## Pause or cancel a plan

Pausing a subscription in Tally changes how Tally tracks it and excludes the paused plan from the month total. It does not pause billing with the provider.

Use the provider cancellation link in the subscription details when one is available, or open the provider's billing settings yourself. Complete cancellation there and retain the confirmation. Apple or Google marketplace subscriptions may need cancellation through the relevant store account.

Deleting a Tally record does not cancel the service, stop a payment or dispute a charge. If a cancellation is followed by another bill, compare the effective cancellation date, account and invoice period with the provider.
